Play hard, but play fair. Each player is strictly limited to one account per universe. Multi-accounting, account sitting without prior admin approval, and "pushing" (using a weaker account solely to funnel resources to a stronger one) will get all involved accounts banned. Do not use bots, scripts, or automation tools that play the game for you. Bashing (attacking the same planet more than six times in a 24-hour period) is prohibited unless your alliances are formally at war. Keep chat and messages focused on the game—trash talk goes with the territory, but real-world harassment, hate speech, or doxxing is an instant permanent ban.
