We collect only the bare minimum data needed to run the game: your email address (for login and password resets), your hashed password, your IP address (for security and multi-account detection), and basic session cookies so you stay logged in. We do not use tracking pixels, third-party analytics, or advertising networks. We will never sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside the server administration team. Server logs are periodically purged. If you want your account and all associated personal data permanently deleted from our database, reach out via the contact details and we will wipe it promptly.
